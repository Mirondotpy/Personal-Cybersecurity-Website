Passwords

Passwords are your first line of defense for an account.

It's important for them to be secure.

  • Mariusz Michalowski from Spacelift highlights

The traditional advice on creating a password isn’t very useful nowadays.

  • Modern-day brute force attacks aren't guessed by humans anymore – instead by computer algorithms which churn through tens of billions of passwords a second
  • “At that speed, your clever substitution of “@” for “a” buys you microseconds of additional security” (Bhargava, 2026)
  • Requiring upper and lower case letters, a number, and a special character doesn't make it harder for machines to guess – just harder for humans to remember.
  • The best security measure is length – the longer the better and at least 16 characters.
