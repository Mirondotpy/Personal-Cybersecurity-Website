With this much reliance on the internet, it’s more important than ever to stay secure.

Many important things are stored online, so if they get compromised or stolen it can lead to catastrophic losses

Most of the world’s population relies heavily on digital services and accounts. Business, infrastructure, and homes are all connected together in the “internet of things,” meaning critical services are often controlled through the internet.

It also affects individuals: “smart homes” have internet-controlled locks, appliances, and more, which can cause huge problems if compromised.


AI is conducting cyberattacks on a larger scale than ever before.

It’s essential to install up-to-date antivirus and security software to block cyberattack attempts.

Make sure to engage in good cybersecurity practices with things like passwords, wifi networks, and sharing data. Strategies for different areas of cybersecurity are outlined in the website’s sections.


Losses due to cyberattacks are steadily climbing.

  • Steve Alder from the HIPAA Journal says that 2025 was a record year for cybercrime losses for the tenth year in a row.
  • Over 1 million complaints were made, and and estimated $20.8 billion in losses. (Alder, 2026)
